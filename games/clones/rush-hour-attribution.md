# Rush Hour puzzle data attribution

The twenty ranked puzzle configurations in `rush-hour.txt` were generated by
Michael Fogleman's exhaustive Rush Hour enumerator. They comprise two of the
hardest 4x4 configurations, the three hardest 5x5 configurations, five
moderate 6x6 configurations, five medium 6x6 configurations, and the five
hardest 6x6 configurations. Fogleman's metric counts a slide of any distance
as one move.

Source: <https://github.com/fogleman/rush>

Article and database documentation: <https://www.michaelfogleman.com/rush/>
