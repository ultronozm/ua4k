TITLE AggreGate

DESCRIPTION S alternates, C is constant, and A groups its four copies into two equal pairs. A commit is atomic: one bad assignment rejects the entire circuit.

TITLE Joined Circuit

DESCRIPTION Solve three joined gates. Outputs are checked together, reflecting the paper's serial circuit discipline: the next deduction is made from the current diagram, not from a bag of remembered equalities.

;; Mark every gate whose toggle partition realizes its advertised assignment.
;; S abstracts the alternating signed-sum behavior; C the constant behavior;
;; A the paired four-copy AggreGate behavior.
CMD mark_gates
 TRY_ALL
  S|1212|Po S|1212|PO

  S|2121|Po S|2121|PO

  C|1111|Po C|1111|PO

  C|2222|Po C|2222|PO

  A|1122|Po A|1122|PO

  A|2211|Po A|2211|PO

  A|1221|Po A|1221|PO

  A|2112|Po A|2112|PO

;; All boards use three aligned outputs.  A successful serial commit joins
;; their witnesses and promotes the last pin to the circuit output Z.
CMD finish_circuit

CMD evaluate

  CALL mark_gates
