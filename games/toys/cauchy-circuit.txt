TITLE Read This First: The Rules

DESCRIPTION A leaf is a slot where one 1-bounded function is evaluated. The root @ stores the variables feeding those slots. One CS(S) keypress means ONE ordinary Cauchy--Schwarz inequality, applied after jointly conditioning on the output values of every leaf in S. S may contain one leaf or many leaves carrying different functions: the product of all selected factors is still 1-bounded. The step makes L and R copies of the whole current diagram; R functions are conjugated. Matching copies of every selected leaf are identified as internal J vertices and cease to be exposed function slots. Each CS changes the outside exponent from 1 to 1/2, then 1/4, and so on. Wrong or out-of-order keys do nothing. Press n when ready.

TITLE First Step: Eliminate f3

DESCRIPTION Start with A = E[z,w] f1(2z-w) f2(z) f3(w), where f1, f2, f3 may all be different and are each 1-bounded. Press 3 for CS({3}). Analytically, this holds the value phi3(v)=w fixed, uses |f3(w)| <= 1, and squares the remaining inner average. Diagrammatically it makes L and conjugated R copies of leaves 1 and 2, while the two copies of vertex 3 are fused into internal J3. The result is |A| <= |B|^(1/2). The checkmark ends this tutorial level only: the U2 bound is NOT proved yet; it needs the second CS step in the next level.

TITLE Both CS Steps: Build D2

DESCRIPTION First press 3. Then press 2 for CS({(L,2),(R,2)}). This second action is ONE Cauchy--Schwarz inequality on the joint outer variable (z1,z2), not two inequalities silently bundled together. It removes the single 1-bounded product f2(z1) bar(f2(z2)). Equivalently, Proposition 2.1.15 permits any subset S and conditions on the tuple of all its output coordinates. The resulting D2 has four roots, four exposed copies of leaf 1, two internal joins inherited from leaf 3, and two new internal joins from the L2 and R2 leaves. LL and RR carry plain f1; LR and RL carry conjugated f1. The exponent is now 1/4.

TITLE The Morphism Is More Than Relabelling

DESCRIPTION The last zero-cost proof step is a genuine diagram morphism Theta: Diag(U2) -> D2, in that direction. It must map every U2 parallelogram (y, y+b, y+a, y+a+b) to four compatible three-term-progression roots, respecting all four exposed leaf-1 values and every internal join. Press m to reveal the explicit maps from the paper. Division by 2 requires odd characteristic. The general entailment formalism concludes with possibly different translates of f1 at 00 and 11 and conjugate translates at 01 and 10; the paper explicitly calls this weaker than the direct calculation and then uses Gowers--Cauchy--Schwarz to recover the usual ||f1||_U2 bound.
